Project Overview

A magnificent 12,000 square foot luxury builder project on prestigious Kalidas Road. This architectural masterpiece combines contemporary design with timeless elegance, featuring expansive living spaces, premium finishes, and thoughtful attention to detail throughout.

The design maximizes natural light and ventilation while creating seamless transitions between indoor and outdoor spaces. Each level has been carefully planned to offer privacy, comfort, and stunning views of the surrounding landscape.
